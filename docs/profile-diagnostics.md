# Profile diagnostics

Thomson scattering, ECE, interferometry and XICS observe kinetic profiles at
points or along chords in real space. TIAGO evaluates them on any plasma state
that can map flux labels to real space, and differentiates them with respect
to the state's parameters and to the profile parameters.

## Provider-neutral state

`real_space_state_t` (`src/diagnostics/real_space_state.f90`) is the seam. A
provider implements, for labels `u = (rho, theta, phi)`,

- `eval(u, x, dx/du, |B|, d|B|/du)`;
- `param_jvp(u, dp) = (dX/dp dp, d|B|/dp dp)` and the transposed
  `param_vjp(u, xbar, bbar)`, at fixed labels;
- `n_params`, `get_params`, `set_params`.

`rho` is the label of the kinetic profiles (`rho = 1` on the boundary);
`theta` and `phi` may be any smooth chart. TIAGO inverts `x = X(u; p)` by a
damped Newton iteration (`locate`) and differentiates the inverse with the
implicit-function theorem,

```text
du = J^{-1} (dx - dX/dp dp),   J = dX/du,
dq = dq/dp dp + grad_u q . du   for q = rho, |B| at a fixed point x.
```

Implementations:

- `vmec_state_t`: VMEC wout, `rho = sqrt(s)`, cylindrical `phi`. Each Fourier
  coefficient of R, Z and |B| is a C1 cubic Hermite interpolant in rho of its
  radial node values, which are the parameters. A VMEC adjoint maps their
  cotangent to its inputs. Stellarator-symmetric equilibria only.
- `circular_state_t`: shifted circles with `|B| = B0 R0 / R`, the analytic
  test state, generated by FortSym.
- A KIN6D real-space state implements the same five procedures; nothing in
  the diagnostics depends on nested Fourier surfaces.

Profiles are `radial_profile_t` (value, rho-derivative, parameter JVP/VJP).
`node_profile_t` is the reference implementation; the latent parameterizations
of the inference layer extend the same type.

## Forward models

**Thomson scattering.** Each channel is a scattering volume of length `L` along
the laser, sampled by Gauss-Legendre points with normalized weights `w_k`:
`ne = sum w_k ne(rho_k)` and `Te = sum w_k ne_k Te_k / ne`, because each part of
the volume scatters in proportion to its density. Points outside the plasma
contribute nothing, and the motion of the boundary across a volume is not
differentiated, so edge channels should lie inside the boundary.

**ECE, cold resonance.** Channel `i` with frequency `f_i`, harmonic `n_i` and a
line of sight from the antenna reports `Te(rho_res)` at the first point inside
the plasma where `n_i e |B| / (2 pi m_e) = f_i`. With
`g(t, p) = |B|(x(t); p) - B_res = 0`, the resonance moves by
`dt = (dB_res - d|B|_x) / (d|B|/dt)`; `ece_frequency_jacobian` gives the
derivative with respect to each channel frequency for calibration nuisances.

**Interferometer and XICS.** A chord is scanned for `rho = 1` crossings, which
are refined by bisection; each plasma segment carries a Gauss-Legendre rule.
The interferometer returns `int ne dl`; the XICS-like model returns the
brightness `int eps dl` and `int eps T dl / int eps dl` for an emissivity
profile `eps` and a temperature profile `T` (Te or Ti).

## Derivatives

Every model has a JVP and a VJP for the state parameters and for each profile.
They differentiate the discrete model: sample labels move through the implicit
inverse, chord samples move with the crossings
(`t_k = t_a + (t_b - t_a) xi_k`), and the segment weights change with the
crossing positions. Locating samples and the reductions of the VJPs are
OpenMP-parallel.

## Extension point: radiation transport for ECE

The cold-resonance model assumes an optically thick, unbroadened emission
layer. A TRAVIS-like model (relativistic and Doppler-broadened absorption and
emission, finite optical depth, refraction) replaces the single resonance by
a weighted ray integral `T_rad = int Te(s) w(s) ds` with the weight
`w = alpha exp(-tau)` from the absorption coefficient `alpha`. Such a model can
reuse `chord_samples` (or a refracted ray), `samples_jvp` and `samples_vjp`
for the geometry, and adds the derivatives of `alpha` with respect to ne, Te
and |B|. It is not implemented.

## Verification

`tiago_profile_diagnostics` checks, on concentric circles with
`ne, Te, eps, Ti` proportional to `1 - rho^2` (reproduced exactly by the
Hermite profiles):

- the inverse map against `rho = sqrt((R - R0)^2 + Z^2) / a`;
- the Thomson volume averages against closed-form polynomial integrals;
- the ECE resonance at `R = n e B0 R0 / (2 pi m_e f)` and `Te` there;
- `int ne dl`, the XICS brightness and weighted temperature on a vertical
  chord against closed-form integrals;
- with a Shafranov shift, every JVP against central finite differences of the
  complete forward model, and every VJP against the transposition identity.

`tiago_profile_diagnostics_vmec` repeats the derivative checks and an
eval/locate round trip on the pinned LI383 VMEC equilibrium.
