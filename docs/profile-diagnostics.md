# Profile diagnostics

Thomson scattering, ECE, interferometry and XICS observe kinetic profiles at
points or along chords in real space. They implement the profile-diagnostic
contract of [docs/inference.md](inference.md) (`profile_diagnostic_t`): each
instrument declares its sample points (R, phi, Z), predicts its signals from
the sampled quantities `q` (s, |B|, ne, Te, Ti, Zeff), and supplies the JVP
and VJP with respect to `q` at fixed points. It never sees the provider's
coordinates or the profile parameterization.

## Sampling a provider state

`real_space_state_t` (`src/diagnostics/real_space_state.f90`) is the provider
seam for the rows s and |B|. A provider implements, for labels
`u = (rho, theta, phi)` with `rho = 1` on the boundary,

- `eval(u, x, dx/du, |B|, d|B|/du)`;
- `param_jvp(u, dp) = (dX/dp dp, d|B|/dp dp)` and the transposed `param_vjp`,
  at fixed labels;
- `n_params`, `get_params`, `set_params`.

TIAGO inverts `x = X(u; p)` by a damped Newton iteration (`locate`) and
differentiates the inverse with the implicit-function theorem,

```text
du = J^{-1} (dx - dX/dp dp),   J = dX/du,
dq = dq/dp dp + grad_u q . du   for q = s = rho^2, |B| at a fixed point x.
```

`tiago_state_sampler` fills `plasma_sample_t` (rows `Q_S`, `Q_B`, their
gradients in (R, phi, Z), `inside`) and propagates `q_dot`/`q_bar` of these
rows to the provider parameters (`sample_state_jvp`, `sample_state_vjp`). The
kinetic rows come from the profiles of the inference core.

Implementations:

- `vmec_state_t`: VMEC wout, `rho = sqrt(s)`, cylindrical `phi`. Each Fourier
  coefficient of R, Z and |B| is a C1 cubic Hermite interpolant in rho of its
  radial node values, which are the parameters; a VMEC adjoint maps their
  cotangent to its inputs. Stellarator-symmetric equilibria only.
- `circular_state_t`: shifted circles with `|B| = B0 R0 / R`, the analytic
  test state, generated by FortSym.
- A KIN6D real-space state implements the same procedures; nothing in the
  instruments or the sampler depends on nested Fourier surfaces, only on a
  label rho with rho <= 1 inside.

## Instruments

**Thomson scattering** (`tiago_thomson_scattering`). Each volume of length
`L` along the laser is sampled by Gauss-Legendre points with normalized
weights: `ne = sum w_k ne_k`, `Te = sum w_k ne_k Te_k / ne`, because each part
of the volume scatters in proportion to its density. An optional rigid
(dR, dZ) shift of all volumes is the alignment nuisance.

**Recorded ECE** (`tiago_ece_radiometer`, `tiago_ece_recorded`). The
[composed model](ece-recorded.md) traces cold rays through the equilibrium,
samples the kinetic profiles, evaluates perpendicular relativistic opacity and
thermal transport, then applies the calibrated receiver. It replaces the cold
resonance interpolation. The composition tape includes moving-ray and
cell-length derivatives as well as the sampled profile contract.

**Interferometer and XICS** (`tiago_line_integrals`). Chords carry composite
Gauss-Legendre rules. The interferometer returns `int ne dl`; the XICS model
returns the brightness `s_i int eps dl` and `int eps T dl / int eps dl` with
`T = Ti` (or Te) and the coronal line emissivity
`eps = ne n_Z exp(-E_line / Te) / sqrt(Te)`, `n_Z = ne (Zeff - 1) / (Z (Z - 1))`
(FortSym kernel `xics_emissivity`). The kinetic quantities vanish outside the
plasma, so the rules need no boundary crossings; the kink of the integrand at
the boundary limits their accuracy to O(h^2) there.

Sampling the state (Newton per point) and the VJP reduction are
OpenMP-parallel; the instruments themselves are cheap sums over the samples.

## Verification

`tiago_profile_instruments` uses kinetic profiles linear in s (ne, Te, Ti
proportional to 1 - s, Zeff = 2) and checks, on concentric circles:

- the inverse map against `rho = sqrt((R - R0)^2 + Z^2) / a`;
- the Thomson volume averages against closed-form polynomial integrals;
- `int ne dl`, the XICS brightness and the emissivity-weighted Ti on a vertical
  chord against closed-form integrals.

For every instrument it then runs the contract's `check_diagnostic_derivatives`
and, with a Shafranov shift, compares the JVP of the complete chain
`state -> sample -> signals` with central finite differences in the state
parameters and checks the transposition identity of the chained VJP.
`tiago_profile_instruments_vmec` repeats the chain checks and an eval/locate
round trip on the pinned LI383 VMEC equilibrium.

The former cold-resonance ECE oracle has been replaced by the full-chain
thick-plasma, derivative, convergence and posterior tests described in
[ece-recorded.md](ece-recorded.md).
